Field ManualHow to Play

1 · Move. Tap a unit — the blue tiles are everywhere it can go this turn. Tap one to move there. Forests/mountains cost more to enter but give cover.

2 · Attack. Enemies in range glow red — tap one to fire without moving, or move first and then attack. A forecast shows the damage dealt and taken, so every shot is a real decision. Out-range them to avoid the counter.

3 · High ground. Stand a Sniper on a Hill (▲) or Mountain (▲▲) and its range extends — snipe from afar while staying out of reach. High ground also gives more cover and sight.

4 · Earn & repair. Buildings pay gold each turn: City +100, HQ +200, Oil Rig +300 — and any unit parked on a building you own heals +2 HP. Select a Sentry (for HQ/cities/barracks) — capturable buildings ring gold ⚑. Move on, pick Capture, and hold the spot until the bar fills (about two turns — leave and it resets). Oil rigs are different: only an Engineer takes them — just move it in and it seizes the rig instantly. Gold builds units at your Barracks.

5 · End Turn. When you're done, End Turn collects your income and lets the enemy move. The button glows when all your units have acted.

Win: capture the enemy HQ (move onto it, Capture twice) or destroy their whole army. Strategy: the player who controls the oil out-builds the other — fight for it.
